About the brand

Physics education with purpose.

Making Physics Simple. Building Future Scientists.

Our mission

Turn “I don’t get it” into “I can solve it.”

Physics With Tshepang exists to make quality physics support clear, accessible and inspiring for Grade 10–12 learners. We combine patient teaching with serious academic structure.

Every lesson is built around understanding, active practice and the confidence to approach unfamiliar questions.

01 / CLARITY

Explain simply

We translate difficult concepts into practical, visual and memorable explanations.

02 / PRACTICE

Practise purposefully

Questions are selected to build reasoning, accuracy and exam technique.

03 / PROGRESS

Grow with confidence

Consistent feedback helps learners see what they know and what to improve next.
